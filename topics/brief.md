# Bio ML KB Brief

This repo is a durable systems map for the parts of computational biology that feel most aligned with Akash's interests: genomics, biological ML, mechanistic or interpretable modeling, virtual-cell directions, and project ideas that connect reading to actual work.

The goal is not just to bookmark papers. It is to keep a high-signal map of:
- what technical directions are real
- which ideas are hype versus solid method shifts
- which topics could turn into strong student projects
- how comp bio and AI systems work can connect in a concrete way
